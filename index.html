Complex Cases, Clinical Radiographs

A curated selection of challenging, high-difficulty clinical cases—not an exhaustive log of routine treatments—highlighting 5-canal and C-shaped molar anatomy, open-apex apical plug creation, separated instrument retrieval, internal root resorption, large apical radiolucencies, and severely curved canals. Each case is detailed by Diagnosis, Challenge, and Management. Click any radiograph to zoom, pan, invert, or adjust diagnostic contrast.

CHALLENGING CASES ONLY

Only selected complex, referral-grade cases are featured here—not every routine endodontic treatment performed in daily clinical practice.

DE-IDENTIFIED · PATIENT CONSENT

All radiographs, CBCT views, and clinical photographs displayed in this archive have been fully de-identified and are presented with informed patient consent for educational, clinical, and specialist referral documentation.

Evidence-Based Endodontics & PhD Research

25-Year Cohort Survival Research & Media

First author and statistical analyst of a 25-year retrospective cohort study analyzing 14,233 teeth treated at Akademie für Zahnärztliche Fortbildung Karlsruhe, conducted as part of doctoral research as a PhD candidate (thesis submitted, defense pending) at Christian-Albrechts-Universität zu Kiel and published in the International Endodontic Journal.
